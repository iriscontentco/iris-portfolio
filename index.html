METHOD

My Client Coordination Approach

Effective client coordination is more than answering questions. It means understanding what the client needs, keeping accurate information, coordinating the right next step, communicating clearly, and following through until the request is completed.

Listen

Understand the client's request and concerns.

→

Record

Capture accurate details and deadlines.

→

Coordinate

Contact the right person or team and arrange next steps.

→

Communicate

Keep the client informed clearly and professionally.

→

Follow Up

Check progress and make sure commitments are completed.

→

Close

Confirm resolution and update the record.
